A letter from my heart

WELCOME TO MY BIRTHDAY 🎂❤️

Today, I don't just celebrate another year of my life; I celebrate God's faithfulness.

This year has been one of the hardest chapters of my life. I lost my father in June, and there are moments when I still struggle to believe he's really gone. Losing him has changed me in ways words cannot fully explain.

But somehow, through the tears, the silence, the questions, and the pain, God has kept me. 🙏🏾

Today, I choose gratitude. Grateful for life. Grateful for my family. Grateful for the people who have stood by me. And above all, grateful to God for giving me another year.

Dad, I wish you were here to see me celebrate this day. ❤️🕊️

Happy birthday to me. 🎂🥂

May this new chapter bring healing, wisdom, purpose, strength, and greater blessings.

Thank You, Lord, for another year. 🙏🏾❤️

✦  🕊️  ✦

Today is my birthday… but my heart is carrying a lot. 🥺❤️

This is my first birthday without my father. I lost him in June, and sometimes it still doesn't feel real. There are moments I wish I could hear his voice, see his smile, or simply have him here to say, "Happy birthday, son." 🕊️

Losing him has reminded me just how fragile and precious life truly is.

I may have lost people I love, but I have not lost my faith. 🙏🏾

Dad, wherever you are, I hope you would be proud of the person I'm becoming. ❤️🕊️

Another year. Another chapter.
Another reason to thank God. 🙏🏾✨
