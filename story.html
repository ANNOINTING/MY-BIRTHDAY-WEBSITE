My Senior Brother, Pastor, Mentor, and Guiding Light

"The Lord bless you and keep you; the Lord make His face shine upon you and be gracious to you; the Lord lift up His countenance upon you and give you peace."

— Numbers 6:24-26

Dear Pastor Stephen,

On this special day that we share, I celebrate not just your birthday, but the incredible gift you are to so many lives — including mine. Your wisdom, faithfulness, and Christ-like leadership have been a beacon of hope and guidance. As my senior brother and spiritual father, you've walked beside me with patience, prayed over me with fervor, and led by example in ways words cannot fully capture.

May this new year of your life be marked by:

  • 🕊️ Fresh anointing and divine direction
  • 🕊️ Strength for every assignment God has entrusted to you
  • 🕊️ Joy unspeakable in your ministry and family
  • 🕊️ Health, longevity, and abundant fruitfulness
  • 🕊️ The continued favor of God upon your life and lineage

Thank you for being a spiritual father who loves like Christ. It's an honor to celebrate this day with you. Happy Birthday, Pastor Stephen. 🎂⛪❤️

With deep love, honor, and gratitude,
Robert Mensah
